# Retaj Alrasheed

**Product & Experience Designer | Software Engineering Background | Creative Technology & Innovation**

I design digital experiences at the intersection of human needs, thoughtful interaction, and technology. My software engineering background helps me work across design and development, connect product decisions to technical realities, and turn early ideas into testable experiences.
