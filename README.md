# Retaj Alrasheed

**Product & Experience Designer | Software Engineering Background | Creative Technology & Innovation**

I design digital experiences at the intersection of human needs, thoughtful interaction, and technology. My software engineering background helps me work across design and development, connect product decisions to technical realities, and turn early ideas into testable experiences.

My practice spans user research, product thinking, interaction design, prototyping, design systems, and cross-functional collaboration. I'm currently expanding my design and technical practice at the **Apple Developer Academy**.

## What I work on

- **Product & experience design** — research, user journeys, interaction design, usability testing, and prototyping
- **Design systems & delivery** — component-based interfaces, accessible experiences, and developer handoff
- **Creative technology** — exploring meaningful applications of emerging technology in everyday life
- **Technical foundations** — software engineering, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and foundational SwiftUI learning

## My approach

**Understand the context → Define the problem → Prototype → Test → Refine.**

I am especially interested in human-centered innovation, sustainability, digital experiences, and the space between design and technology.
